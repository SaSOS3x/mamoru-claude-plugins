**What is going on** - The server is not slow per tick. It stalls between ticks, waiting for chunks. That is why MSPT (median 6 ms, 95th 14 ms) looks healthy while "Can't keep up ... 25000ms" appears twice a minute. MSPT measures only the tick, not the waits. At 30 blocks/s the player outruns generation.
1. Vanilla fall-damage check blocks on the chunk under the player (72%): handleMovePlayer -> doCheckFallDamage -> getFluidState -> getChunkBlocking -> managedBlock -> park. Every wait delays later ticks (backlog, late normal chunks). Vanilla behaviour; levers are pre-generation and faster generation, not client configs.
2. Frontier SnowDriftSystem.tick (9%) forces synchronous chunk loads. Level.isLoaded(pos) in 1.21.1 only means "the chunk has a FULL ticket", not "ready", so getBlockState after it can still generate the chunk on the main thread. Fix: replace the check with level.getChunkSource().getChunkNow(x >> 4, z >> 4) != null and skip the spot if null. Smaller than cause 1 but cheap. A comparable mod (Sengoku WarmWaterSystem) gained about 2 TPS from the same fix, in one run.
3. Distant Horizons is a symptom, not the cause. pauseIfMovingQuickly = false with no change fits. Holes near the edge of generated land mean the server is not supplying chunks. DH can also pause itself on a queue over 1000 or when limitIfServerUnhealthy is on; check that, but expect no effect until the server is fixed.
4. CraterRegistry: 15% of worker self time; contribution() loops over every crater ever added; add() also on load from disk; cleared only at server start; cost grows with the world, Chunky slows the longer you play; hurts pre-generation. Fix: spatial index keyed by chunk (Long2ObjectMap, ChunkPos.asLong); add() puts each crater into every chunk its influence radius touches; contribution reads only that chunk's list (or 3x3); add() idempotent by position or piece id; thread-safe for C2ME workers. Not a config issue.
**What to do, in order** 1. Pre-generate with Chunky where you fly (biggest lever), with the CraterRegistry fix first. 2. Fix SnowDriftSystem (getChunkNow). 3. Fix CraterRegistry. 4. Optionally fly slower into new terrain. Do not expect DH settings to help.
**How to measure honestly** Trust: real TPS from timestamps of the Can't keep up lines (N is backlog since last warning; MSPT ~ 50 + N/300; 25000 ms -> ~133 ms, ~7.5 TPS); count of Can't keep up per minute on the same route; profile shares, not absolute numbers; for the crater fix: share of worker time in contribution and Chunky chunks/s at the same radius and world age. Do not trust: median/95th MSPT from /spark tps (blind to waits outside the tick); TPS while the profiler runs (spark's sampler slows the JVM); comparisons between pre-generated and new worlds. Method: same route, speed, seed, fresh terrain each run (copy of the world), one change per test, nothing heavy on the PC, server-thread profile without --thread *, separate --thread * profile for workers, repeat each variant at least twice. Expected for the SnowDrift fix (assumption): its 9% disappears, getChunkBlocking shrinks a bit, cause 1 stays until pre-generation. Report real TPS before/after, Can't keep up per minute, top frames with spark link, Chunky chunks/s.
